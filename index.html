Background

The architecture of good work.

My approach to work wasn't shaped in a vacuum. It grew from a persistent curiosity about how systems connect—how human behavior, technical constraints, and business goals constantly pull at one another.

Early in my career, I realized that the most beautiful strategy is entirely useless if it cannot be operationalized. Since then, I have dedicated myself to the space where planning meets reality. I act as a translator across disciplines, ensuring that visionary ideas survive the friction of implementation.

I don't just want to know what we are building; I want to understand why it matters, who it serves, and how we measure its truth.
